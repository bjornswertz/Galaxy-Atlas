Mass is given in solar masses (M☉). The event horizon radius is the Schwarzschild radius for a non-spinning black hole, 2.95 km per solar mass. 1 AU is the distance from the Earth to the Sun (150 million km). Black holes themselves are invisible. In the Telescope column, mag is the apparent brightness of the object you can actually see (a quasar, a companion star or the host galaxy), not of the black hole, and the months show when it stands high enough at night from the Netherlands. Long-exposure photos (for example with a smart telescope) reach much fainter objects than the eye. Black holes themselves are invisible: a few have a bright companion star or surrounding glow that you can see in a telescope, which is mentioned in the notes.
