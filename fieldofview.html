🔭 Field of view

How much of your frame does a galaxy fill? Choose your frame size and see every galaxy with a known size, drawn to scale on a real sky photo. Sorted by the best time of the year, then from large to small.

Fill is the length of the galaxy compared with the long side of the frame. Sizes are rounded catalogue values for the visible disk, so a photo usually shows a little less than this. Only galaxies with a known size are listed. Photos: DSS2 via CDS (internet required). Best months are calculated for the Netherlands (52.2°N, 5.3°E).
