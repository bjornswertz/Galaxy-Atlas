⏳ Time Dilation Lab

Time does not tick at the same rate everywhere. Near heavy objects, and when you move very fast, your clock runs slower than a clock far away. Pick a place or a speed and see the difference.

Gravity: for a non-rotating mass and an observer standing still at that distance, your clock runs at √(1 − rs/r) of the rate far away, where rs = 2GM/c² is the event horizon radius. Speed: your clock runs at √(1 − v²/c²) of the rate of people at rest (special relativity). Earth and Sun are compared with a clock infinitely far away, and their rotation and motion are ignored. Trips ignore the time spent speeding up and slowing down. These are simplified textbook formulas, not a full simulation.
